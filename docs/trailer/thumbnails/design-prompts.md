The generated originals were 1672 × 941. Delivery exports fit them to exact 16:9 at 3840 × 2160 and 1280 × 720, without changing the creative image content. JPEGs remain below 2,000,000 bytes.

Keep lettering fully inside safe margins of at least 150 pixels. Keep important subject and words clear of bottom-right 450 x 180 pixels for the YouTube duration badge. Constraints: no other vehicles, no new features, no people, no extra slogans or captions, no website/interface, no Android or YouTube logos, no invented car sponsor logos, no watermarks, no border. This is illustrated marketing art based on the actual game references.
